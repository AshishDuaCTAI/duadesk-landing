THE OPERATING MODEL

From enterprise signals to controlled action.

Every command centre uses the same underlying pattern: connect to the existing estate, compute from evidence, reason through the problem, prove the result and keep a human decision gate where professional judgement is required.

01 · CONNECT

Enterprise signals

ERP, accounting, banks, GST, statutory sources, documents, spreadsheets, market and regulatory feeds. Read-only where possible. No rip-and-replace starting point.

02 · COMPUTE

Deterministic work

Reconciliation, calculations, population testing, controls, classifications, schedules and source-row checks are computed rather than guessed.

03 · REASON

Agent workforce

Specialist agents interpret the evidence, challenge hypotheses, identify exceptions, prepare outputs and hand off only what requires human judgement.

04 · PROVE

Trust ledger

Source-stamped figures, confidence/provisionality, audit trail, escalation and four-eyes sign-gates turn AI output into reviewable enterprise work.

TRUST IS PART OF THE PRODUCT

Autonomy without surrendering control.

AI does the repetitive analysis and drafting. Filing, posting, payments and professional judgement stay behind a human gate.

Evidence before confidence

Source-stamped evidence — findings trace back to source rows, documents or external corroboration.
Deterministic computation — code-based calculations and invariant checks where appropriate.
Full-population testing — where the product capability supports it, the system works across the population rather than relying only on a sample.
Provisionality — AI-drafted outputs are distinguished from reviewed/signed outputs.

Action behind a gate

Four-eyes approval — professional review/sign-off before consequential output.
Trust Ledger — draft → advised → signed state.
Escalation — exceptions move to the human rather than being silently resolved.
No invented figure — every output is grounded in your connected source data.
THE AUTONOMY PATH

More autonomy. More control.

DuaDesk can progressively move from assistance toward bounded execution. The control model does not disappear as autonomy increases; it becomes explicit in permissions, policies, evidence, escalation and auditability.

LEVEL 01

Assist

AI reads, extracts, reconciles, analyses and drafts the work.

Human: decides and executes.
LEVEL 02

Recommend

AI reasons over evidence, identifies exceptions and proposes the next action.

Human: approves the recommendation.
LEVEL 03

Controlled execution

AI prepares a transaction or action inside an explicit workflow and policy boundary.

Control: sign-gate before consequential execution.
LEVEL 04

Bounded autonomy

Low-risk, pre-authorised actions can run within defined limits, with monitoring and escalation.

Control: permissions, thresholds, logs and exception escalation.
The principle: automate the repetitive work progressively — never hide the control boundary.
