Leaders come and go; the community stays. DuaDesk helps it be heard, served and kept safe, with people in charge.

Present · Purpose · Possibility

Who does what, at the top

The same three steps run in every DuaDesk product. Here they are at the scale of social leadership: what people bring, and what AI does.

What is

Present

What people bring

  • Residents’ own reports and sense of how their community is doing
  • Leaders’ knowledge of people, places and history

What AI does

  • Keeps the community’s memory: promises, decisions and outcomes across changes of leaders
  • Turns every report into a tracked fact, never a profile of a person

What should be

Purpose

What people bring

  • Shared values: fairness, safety, cleanliness and dignity for every resident
  • Priorities agreed by the community, through its leaders and its polls

What AI does

  • Shows where actions drift from those values, including for residents who are not on the app

What could be

Possibility

What people bring

  • What kind of community people want to become

What AI does

  • Brings forward collective possibilities for leaders and residents to consider
