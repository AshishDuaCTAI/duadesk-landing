P³ · For business

The Organisation

An organisation outlives every CEO. DuaDesk helps it remember, decide and grow, with people in charge.

The CEO is the bridge between the desks below and the Board above. Audit and advisory stay outside the line, so they remain independent.

What lasts

The organisation: its purpose, its people, its stakeholders and its continuity.

Who serves it

The Board and owners hold its purpose; the CEO and every function head serve it for a time.

Present · Purpose · Possibility

Who does what, at the top

The same three steps run in every DuaDesk product. Here they are at the scale of organisation: what people bring, and what AI does.

What is

Present

What people bring

  • The Board’s and owners’ judgement of how the organisation is doing for everyone it serves
  • Experience of the market, people and history no system records

What AI does

  • Keeps the organisation’s memory: decisions, reasons and outcomes, across changes of CEO
  • Brings every product’s view of the present into one honest picture, with sources

What should be

Purpose

What people bring

  • Purpose, values, risk appetite and succession
  • What the organisation will never do

What AI does

  • Shows where the direction drifts from the stated purpose
  • Never sets the purpose

What could be

Possibility

What people bring

  • What the organisation should become over decades

What AI does

  • Brings forward long-range possibilities for the Board to weigh, with risks and costs
