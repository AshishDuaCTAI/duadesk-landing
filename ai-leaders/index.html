Questions

Frequently asked

What levels do you recruit for?

Senior leadership: C-suite, global and regional heads, and leaders of AI functions at capability centres, typically with 15 or more years of experience.

Do you only work with technology companies?

No. We work with companies where AI is changing the core business: banks, insurers, manufacturers, consumer brands and global capability centres.

How do you assess AI leaders?

On evidence. We look for AI systems in production, measurable business value, the size and quality of the teams built, and how risk and regulation were handled.

Do you use AI in your search?

Yes, to map the market and organise research faster. Every assessment, conversation and recommendation is made by a person.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. We never share a candidate’s profile with a client without their agreement, and we keep client searches confidential. Candidates are never charged to be considered for a search.

Can you help us define the role before we hire?

Yes. Many clients start with a role-definition session. It is often the most valuable part of the search.
