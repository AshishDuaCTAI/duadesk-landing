Send thank-you and missed-you emails

Attendees get: recording link, attendance certificate, feedback form, “talk to us”. People who registered but didn’t attend get: “sorry we missed you” with the recording. Each person gets it once.

People & WhatsApp

Upload contacts (Excel or CSV)

Upload one file at a time, as often as you like. Each file is cleaned before loading: emails are tidied and typo-fixed (e.g. gmial.com → gmail.com), broken emails are dropped, mobiles are converted to +91 format, cells with several emails or mobiles are split into separate people, duplicates are merged, and everyone is labelled by role, industry and city. Emails are then checked against the internet (does the company’s mail server exist?). Anyone who unsubscribed or was deleted stays blocked. PDFs: open them in Excel or send them to Claude to convert first.

WhatsApp, the free way

WhatsApp only lets you message people who opted in. These tools keep you within the rules at no cost:

1 · Broadcast list of opted-in people

People who ticked “WhatsApp reminders” or messaged you. Download, add them to a broadcast list in the WhatsApp Business app (they must have saved your number).

2 · Personal invitations to people you know

20–30 a day, one by one from your phone. Each button opens WhatsApp with the message ready; you check and press send.

Find a person

Use this when someone replies to an outreach email (Replied: make warm), replies STOP, asks to be removed, or messages you on WhatsApp. Tick people and use Find email with RocketReach to fill missing or bad emails (uses RocketReach credits).
