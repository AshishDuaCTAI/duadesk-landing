P³ · For family

The Life Journey

We come into this world as individuals and we leave as individuals. DuaDesk keeps each life’s story with the person whose life it is.

The person whose life it is leads, even when someone else is the user or the payer.

What lasts

The individual, from childhood to legacy.

Who serves it

Parents, partners, children and carers walk alongside for a time; the life belongs to the individual.

Present · Purpose · Possibility

Who does what, at the top

The same three steps run in every DuaDesk product. Here they are at the scale of a whole life: what people bring, and what AI does.

What is

Present

What people bring

  • The person’s own account of their life so far, in their own words

What AI does

  • Keeps each stage’s record safe and separate until the person chooses to connect them
  • Never infers, scores or diagnoses anything about a person

What should be

Purpose

What people bring

  • The person’s own values about how to live: chosen, never prescribed

What AI does

  • Reflects those values back; never tells anyone how to live

What could be

Possibility

What people bring

  • What the person hopes their life will mean

What AI does

  • Helps them notice possibilities they may want, within their own values

When we bring it to you

  1. PresentAt the first home visit we meet your parent, record their history and set up devices, with their consent.
  2. PurposeYour parent’s wishes come first and are written down with them; the family and the clinician add theirs.
  3. PossibilityThe care team reviews early-warning signs and ideas to stay connected, and decides with your parent.
